## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Understand Foundational Concepts of Physical AI (Priority: P1)

The reader engages with Part 1 of the textbook, gaining a high-level understanding of what Physical AI is, how it differs from traditional AI, and its key features. They also get an overview of what to expect in subsequent chapters of this part.

**Why this priority**: This forms the fundamental conceptual groundwork for understanding Physical AI before delving into its historical development or specific turning points. It ensures readers are equipped with the basic definitions and scope.

**Independent Test**: A reader can complete Part 1 and articulate a clear definition of Physical AI, identify its distinct characteristics from conventional AI, and recall the key features discussed. They should also be able to summarize the anticipated content of Chapter 01 and Chapter 02.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a reader starts Part 1, **When** they complete the introductory sections, **Then** they can define Physical AI and differentiate it from general Artificial Intelligence.
2. **Given** a reader starts Part 1, **When** they complete the introductory sections, **Then** they can list the key features and characteristics of Physical AI.
3. **Given** a reader starts Part 1, **When** they complete the introductory sections, **Then** they have a clear understanding of the learning objectives for Chapter 01 and Chapter 02.

---

### User Story 2 - Grasp the Evolution of Physical AI (Priority: P2)

The reader studies Chapter 01 of Part 1, absorbing a high-level overview of the evolution of Physical AI, understanding its key historical aspects and developmental stages.

**Why this priority**: Understanding the historical context provides a crucial foundation for appreciating current advancements and future directions in Physical AI.

**Independent Test**: A reader can complete Chapter 01 and provide a concise overview of the major evolutionary stages and key aspects of Physical AI's development.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a reader starts Chapter 01, **When** they complete it, **Then** they can describe the general evolution of Physical AI.
2. **Given** a reader starts Chapter 01, **When** they complete it, **Then** they can identify key historical aspects or milestones in Physical AI's development.

---

### User Story 3 - Identify Key Turning Points in Physical AI (Priority: P2)

The reader studies Chapter 02 of Part 1, recognizing the significant turning points in Physical AI and understanding their impact on the field's progression.

**Why this priority**: Identifying turning points helps readers understand critical breakthroughs and shifts that shaped the trajectory of Physical AI.

**Independent Test**: A reader can complete Chapter 02 and summarize the major turning points in Physical AI and explain their significance.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a reader starts Chapter 02, **When** they complete it, **Then** they can list key turning points in Physical AI.
2. **Given** a reader starts Chapter 02, **When** they complete it, **Then** they can explain the high-level impact of these turning points on the field.

---
